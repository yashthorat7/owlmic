How to Set Up in 30 Seconds
1
Run Setup
Double-click Owlmic-Setup-0.1.0.exe. Click Install. It configures the virtual mic and camera in ~15 seconds.
2
Allow Driver (UAC)
When Windows prompts to configure Owlmic Mic and Firewall rules, click Yes.
3
Connect & Stream
Launch Owlmic on your phone. Plug via USB cable or join the same Wi-Fi. Click Allow on PC to begin!
